HOW TO USE IT

KEEPER OF THE TOME: GOURAV RATHOD

The Primary Law: The human whose name is written in this notebook shall die.
Face and Name Requirement: The writer must have the victim’s face in mind while writing their name. People sharing the same name are unaffected.
Cause Before Name: A writer can write the cause and scenario of death first, and insert a name at the start of the text later. The name must be entered within 19 calendar days for the conditions to apply.
Age Minimum: The Death Note will not affect anyone under 780 days (approximately 2 years and 50 days) old.
Age Maximum: The Death Note will not affect anyone aged 124 years or older.
Remaining Lifespan: The Death Note will not affect a person with less than 12 minutes of natural lifespan remaining.
Accidental Misspellings: If a victim's name is accidentally misspelled 4 times, that individual becomes permanently immune to the Death Note.
Intentional Misspelling Penalty: If a writer intentionally misspells a name 4 times to grant someone immunity, the writer immediately dies, and the intended victim is not granted immunity.
